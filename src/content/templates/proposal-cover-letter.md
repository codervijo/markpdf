---
title: Proposal Cover Letter Template
description: A one-page proposal cover letter template in markdown. Lead with the client's problem, state your answer and price, and ask for the next step.
docType: Letter
audience: Consultants, freelancers and small agencies sending a proposal or answering an RFP
whenToUse: Send it on top of a formal proposal, especially one answering a request for proposal, when the decision-maker may read only the first page.

**[Your company name]**
[Address] · [Email] · [Phone]

[Date]

[Recipient name]
[Title], [Client company]

**Re: Proposal for [project name] [— RFP reference, if any]**

Dear [Recipient name],

[One or two sentences on the client's problem, in their words. If they sent an
RFP, quote the outcome it asks for. Do not open with "We are pleased to submit".]

[One or two sentences on what you propose to do about it, and the result they
get. Name the single most important deliverable.]

[Why you: one or two specific proof points — a similar project, a measurable
result, a relevant credential. Skip anything generic.]

The enclosed proposal covers scope, timeline and terms. In short:

- **Investment:** [fixed fee / range]
- **Timeline:** [start date] to [end date]
- **Proposal valid until:** [date]

[The next step you want: a call on a specific day, a signature, or questions
by a date. Make it one action.]

Sincerely,

[Your name]
[Title]
[Direct email · phone]

Enclosure: [Proposal title]
