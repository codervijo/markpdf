## Executive summary

[Two or three sentences a busy decision-maker can read on their own. Name the
problem in their words, state the outcome you'll deliver, and the headline
timeline and price.]

> Example: Northwind Labs needs a customer dashboard its support team can use
> without engineering help. This proposal covers design, build, and handoff of
> that dashboard over six weeks for a fixed fee of $24,000.

## Background & understanding

[Show you understand the situation. Summarize what you heard on the call: the
current state, what's not working, and why it matters now. Two short
paragraphs. This is what separates a proposal from a price list — it proves you
were listening.]
