## Overview

[Two or three sentences: the problem the client has, and what you'll deliver to
solve it. Write it so someone who missed the call still understands the plan.]

## What you'll get

- [Deliverable 1 — the concrete thing, not the activity]
- [Deliverable 2]
- [Deliverable 3]

## How I'll work

[A short paragraph on your process and check-ins — e.g. "We'll have a kickoff
call, I'll share progress at the midpoint, and you'll get one revision round
before final delivery."]

## Not included

- [State what's out of scope so expectations are clear — e.g. additional rounds,
  extra deliverables, ongoing work after delivery.]

## Timeline

| Milestone | Target date |
|---|---|
| Kickoff | [Date] |
| First draft / midpoint | [Date] |
| Final delivery | [Date] |

## Price

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| [Project fee] | [$2,500] |
| [Optional add-on] | [$500] |
| **Total** | **[$2,500]** |

**Payment terms:** [50%] to start, [50%] on delivery. Invoices due within
[14 days]. [Optional: a [X]% late fee applies after [15] days.]

## Assumptions

- [What the price depends on — e.g. client provides source materials, brand

## Terms

- **Revisions:** [One round] is included; additional revisions at [$80/hour].
- **Ownership:** Final deliverables transfer to the client on full payment.
- **Cancellation:** Either party may cancel in writing; work completed to date
  is billable.
